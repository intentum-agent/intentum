Initialize intentum if needed, then establish a concise product charter from the repository.

Use the injected repository evidence first. Open files only to fill gaps. Draft users, outcome, constraints, and success criteria from that evidence — do not treat an existing tree as a blank product.

Follow Reflect → Identify uncertainty → Recommend → Ask/Act. Prefer acting: write the draft charter (and architecture direction) with intentum_project. Ask a question only if evidence is missing or contradictory, and ask at most one. Prefer confirming a concrete draft over "who is this for?"
